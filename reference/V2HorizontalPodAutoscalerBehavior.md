# V2HorizontalPodAutoscalerBehavior

V2HorizontalPodAutoscalerBehavior

V2HorizontalPodAutoscalerBehavior

## Details

HorizontalPodAutoscalerBehavior configures the scaling behavior of the
target in both Up and Down directions (scaleUp and scaleDown fields
respectively).
